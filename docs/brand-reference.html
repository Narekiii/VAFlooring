V & A Flooring
Brand Reference

Logo system, color and typography for the V & A Flooring website and related materials. The identity is meant to feel established, architectural and approachable: confident navy type on warm, light grounds, with brass used sparingly.

Logo

The monogram joins a V and an A through one shared stroke. Strokes follow classic serif contrast: the two heavy down-strokes are thick, and the shared up-stroke is thin, so it reads correctly as part of both letters. Every terminal is cut flat and aligned to two horizontal lines, like plank ends on a floor, and the A’s crossbar is a single short plank in brass. The wordmark is set in Libre Baskerville Bold with a smaller, lighter ampersand.

Supporting line

Family Owned • Since 1999

Set separately from the logo, never locked inside it.

Do

  • Use the navy logo on ivory, white or sand.
  • Use the ivory logo on navy or on calm, dark photography.
  • Use the single-color versions for embroidery, stamps and one-color print.

Don’t

  • Recolor, stretch, outline or add effects.
  • Rebuild the wordmark in another typeface or enlarge the ampersand.
  • Place the full-color logo on busy photography.
  • Combine it with Lowe’s or manufacturer logos.

Imagery

Warm, natural architectural photography where the floor is clearly visible. Use V & A Flooring’s own project and crew photography whenever it exists. Any non-company image must be labelled as illustrative and never presented as completed V & A Flooring work.

Files

All logo artwork is flat vector SVG in public/brand/. Favicons are in src/app/. The geometry is generated by design-source/logo/build_logo.py, so proportions can be adjusted and every file rebuilt consistently.
